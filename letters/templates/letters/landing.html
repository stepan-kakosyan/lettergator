{% trans "Everything users usually ask before sending their first future letter." %}

1. How much does LetterGator cost, and when is it charged? + -

Scheduling a letter for under 1 year is free. For schedules at 1 year or beyond, the price is $0.50 per year. The total is charged once when you click Seal and Store, not every month. You can always write letters without paying; payment is only required for long-term scheduling. To unlock 1 year+ scheduling options, keep at least $1.00 in your balance.

2. How reliable is delivery, and what backup details can I provide? + -

LetterGator is built for reliable future delivery with retry logic if a first attempt fails. You can send to yourself or to other recipients, and add up to 5 recipient emails. For additional account-level safety, you may provide a phone number and address in your profile. If provided, those details help us keep your account contact information complete for exceptional support and verification situations.

3. Can I change, delete, or preview a letter after I create it? + -

You control this at creation time. If you enable Allow Edit and/or Allow Delete, those actions are available only during the first 30 days after the letter is created. If you leave either option disabled, that action is not available after creation at all. Message preview works the same way: if See Message Before Delivery is enabled, you can view your content before delivery; if disabled, the content stays sealed until delivery.

4. How do you make sure my email is still valid in future years? + -

To keep long-term delivery dependable, we send a re-confirmation email roughly once a year so you can confirm your address and stay connected with us. This also acts as a reminder to update your account details if your email changes. If you plan to switch email providers, you can update your email in your profile and confirm the new address so future letters continue to route correctly.

5. Is my message content private, and what happens if your database is lost? + -

Every message is encrypted before it is stored. The content is unreadable to anyone who has access to our database — it can only be decrypted at the moment of delivery. For permanent resilience, the encrypted message data is also written to Arweave — a decentralised blockchain storage network built to keep data available permanently. Even if our own database were ever lost or destroyed, the encrypted letter data is preserved on the blockchain and cannot disappear.

6. Can I attach files to a letter? + -

Not yet — letters currently support a rich text body only. File attachments are planned for a near-future version. You will be able to attach videos, audio recordings, images, and other files directly to your letter so the recipient receives everything in one delivery. We will announce this feature as soon as it is available.
