{% trans "How LetterGator handles your data" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} This notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using LetterGator. We designed the service to keep letter content protected while still letting us run the platform, provide delivery, prevent abuse, and support your account. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "1. Information we collect" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, address, letter content, delivery dates, recipient details, attachments, account notes, and support requests. We also collect technical information such as device data, log data, usage events, and other information needed to keep the platform secure and reliable. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2. How we use information" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We use information to create and manage your account, process and deliver letters, verify addresses, send notifications, provide customer support, detect fraud or abuse, improve the product, and comply with legal or accounting obligations. We may also use aggregated or de-identified information for analytics and service improvements. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3. Letter content and encryption" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Letter content is encrypted before it is stored. Decryption is performed only inside controlled application flows when required to render, validate, or deliver a letter. We do not intend for plaintext letter content to be exposed through normal database access alone. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "4. Sharing and disclosure" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers that help us host the application, send email, store backups, process payments, or monitor security. We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect users, our systems, or the public. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "5. Retention and backups" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We retain account data, letter data, payment records, and support history for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Some encrypted data may be backed up or archived to improve durability and recovery options. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "6. Your choices and controls" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} You can update certain profile details in your account, such as your email address, phone number, and address where available. You may also contact us with questions about your data, request help with account access, or ask about deletion options where legally and operationally possible. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "7. Security" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration of data. No system is completely secure, so you should use a strong password, keep your account credentials private, and notify us quickly if you suspect unauthorized access. {% endblocktrans %}
