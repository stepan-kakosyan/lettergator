{% trans "Physical Letters" %}

{% trans "From message to mailbox" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Physical letters combine your message with attachments (documents and photos), then get printed and mailed to the recipient's address. Here's how the process works. {% endblocktrans %}

1

{% trans "Compose with attachments" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Write your message and optionally attach files: up to 3 documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT) and up to 3 photos (JPG, PNG). Everything will be printed together in one envelope. {% endblocktrans %}

2

{% trans "Add recipient address" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Enter the recipient's name, street address, city, state/province, postal code, and country. The price is calculated based on your document pages, photos, and destination country. {% endblocktrans %}

3

{% trans "Set desired delivery date" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Choose the date you'd like the letter to arrive. We'll print it on your requested date and begin shipping. You can schedule up to 10 years in advance. {% endblocktrans %}

4

{% trans "Pay and confirm" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The total price is deducted from your account balance when you confirm. Your account balance must cover the full cost of the letter. {% endblocktrans %}

5

{% trans "Print and ship" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} On your requested delivery date, the letter is printed and shipped via postal service. Delivery times vary by country (typically 2-6 weeks). Track its status in your account dashboard. {% endblocktrans %}

6

{% trans "Recipient receives it" %}

{% blocktrans trimmed %} The letter arrives in your recipient's mailbox — a tangible, memorable keepsake that they can hold in their hands and share with others. {% endblocktrans %}
