How it works

Four steps. One future message.

From the moment you write to the moment it arrives — here is exactly what happens to your letter along the way.

1

Compose

Create a meaningful letter in minutes.

Write your subject line and full message body. There are no character limits — pour in as much as you need. The letter is immediately attached to your account, safely stored, and ready for the next step.

  • Rich message body with full text support
  • Clear subject line for recipient context
  • Auto-saved to your account dashboard
2

Seal

Choose privacy and ownership controls.

Before scheduling, decide the rules that govern your letter. Can you peek at the content before delivery? Should edits be blocked once scheduled? These controls lock in your intentions and prevent second-guessing.

  • Toggle content preview on or off
  • Allow or block edits in the first month
  • Enable or disable early deletion
3

Schedule

Set exact timing for emotional impact.

Pick the precise date and time your message should land. Use quick-select presets — 1 month, 1 year, 5 years, 10 years — or dial in a custom moment: a birthday, anniversary, or milestone you already know is coming.

  • Preset slots for common future milestones
  • Custom date-and-time picker for exact moments
  • Delivery time locked after confirmation
4

Deliver

Automatic delivery with fallback reliability.

When the clock hits your chosen moment, LetterGator dispatches your message to every selected recipient simultaneously. Built-in retry logic protects against delivery failures so your words arrive — no matter what happens between now and then.

  • Sends to all selected recipients at once
  • Retry logic guards against delivery failures
  • Confirmation logged to your account history
