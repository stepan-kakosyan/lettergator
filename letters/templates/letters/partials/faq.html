{% trans "1. How much does LetterGator cost, and when is it charged?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: Delivery under 1 year is free. At 1 year or beyond, the cost is $0.50 per year, charged once when you save the letter. To unlock 1 year+ scheduling, keep at least $1.00 in your balance.

Physical letters: Pricing includes the destination country's base cost, $0.50 per printable page, $0.50 per photo, and $0.50 per year into the future. The full amount is charged once when you pay and submit the order. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "2. How reliable is delivery, and what backup details can I provide?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: Delivery includes retry logic if a first email attempt fails. You can send to yourself or to other recipients, and add up to 5 recipient emails per letter.

Physical letters: Delivery depends on the recipient address, destination country, and postal service timing. Your account details, plus the full mailing address you enter for the order, help us prepare and route the letter correctly. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3. Can I change, delete, or preview a letter after I create it?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: You control edit, delete, and preview rules at creation time. If Allow Edit and/or Allow Delete are enabled, those actions are available only during the first 30 days. Preview works the same way: if See Message Before Delivery is enabled, you can view the message before delivery.

Physical letters: You can save drafts before paying. Paid physical letters can still be updated while they remain in Paid status, and you can delete Draft or Paid letters. Physical letters do not use the same preview controls as email letters because they are prepared for printing and shipping. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "4. How do you make sure my email is still valid in future years?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: We send a re-confirmation email roughly once a year so you can confirm your address and update your account if your email ever changes.

Physical letters: The most important detail is the recipient's mailing address on the order. Review the address carefully before paying, and if you need to change a paid physical letter later, update it before the letter moves beyond Paid status. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "5. Is my message content private, and what happens if your database is lost?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: Message content is encrypted before storage and written to Arweave for long-term resilience. Even if our own database were lost, the encrypted email-letter payload remains preserved.

Physical letters: Your message text, uploaded files, and shipping details are stored with the order so the letter can be prepared for printing and mailing. Physical letters follow a different operational flow than sealed email delivery, so they are not handled with the same delivery-only decryption model. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "6. What's the difference between email letters and physical letters?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email Letters: Scheduled messages that arrive in an inbox at a specific date and time. You choose when to deliver (up to 10 years ahead), and can optionally edit or delete during the first 30 days. Perfect for digital-only messages and milestone reminders.

Physical Letters: Printed and mailed to a recipient's address. You select a desired delivery date, and can attach documents (PDFs, TXT) and photos (JPG, PNG) that will be printed with your message. Perfect for tangible keepsakes, gifts, and memorable occasions. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "7. Can I attach files to email letters?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} No, email letters support only text content. However, if you want to share documents, images, or photos, you can create a physical letter instead, which supports attaching PDFs, Word documents, text files, and photos that will be printed together. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "8. What file types can I attach to physical letters?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Documents: PDF, Word (.DOCX), and Text (.TXT) files are supported and will be printed.
Photos: JPG and PNG images are supported and will be printed in color or black & white.
You can attach up to 3 documents and 3 photos per letter. Each page of your documents is calculated into the total price. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "9. How are refunds handled if I delete a letter?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: Free letters have no refund because no payment was made. Paid email letters keep a fixed $0.50 processing fee, and the rest is returned to your balance if deletion is still allowed.

Physical letters: Drafts have no refund because they were never paid. Paid physical letters refund the remaining balance after a deletion fee of $1.00 plus $0.50 for each full year since the order was created. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "10. How long does delivery take for each letter type?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Email letters: Delivery happens at the exact future date and time you choose.

Physical letters: You choose a requested delivery date, and the order is then prepared for shipping. Delivery depends on the destination country and can occur around 1 to 4 days after your requested date, with later arrival possible depending on postal conditions. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "11. Can I save a physical letter as a draft before paying?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Yes. Physical letters can be saved as drafts first, which is useful when you still need to refine the address, message, or attachments. Drafts are unpaid. Once you are ready, you can return to the draft, review the final price, and pay to move it into Paid status. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "12. Can I track the status of a physical letter after I pay?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Yes. In your Letters workspace, physical letters move through statuses such as Draft, Paid, Printing, Shipped, and Delivered. This helps you understand whether the order is still editable, being prepared, or already on its way. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "13. How do I choose between an email letter and a physical letter?" %} + -

{% blocktrans trimmed %} Choose email letters when precise timing, privacy controls, and inbox delivery matter most. Choose physical letters when you want a tangible keepsake, printed photos or documents, and a more ceremonial mailbox experience for the recipient. {% endblocktrans %}
